1. **Repetition Problem:** Same quotes and ideas being recycled across different scenarios
2. **Quality of Input Sensitivity:** Responses seem to struggle when given limited or low-quality input
3. **Balance Issue:** Need better balance between being supportive and being realistic

5. **UX Issue:** Need to auto-scroll to top when practice plan is generated
